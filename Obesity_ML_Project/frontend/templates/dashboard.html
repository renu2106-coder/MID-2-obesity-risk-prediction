Dataset Provenance & Synthesis Ratio UCI ML Repo #544
Estimation of Obesity Levels Based on Eating Habits and Physical Condition

The study dataset comprises 2,111 records and 16 predictive attributes collected across Mexico, Peru, and Colombia.

Synthetic Sampling Notice: Exactly 498 records (23.6%) were acquired directly from users via web surveys, while 1,613 records (76.4%) were synthetically generated using the SMOTE filter in Weka to overcome class imbalance.
Study Population: Individuals aged 14 to 61 years
Target Variable: NObeyesdad (7 Multiclass Tiers)
Feature Domains: Demographics, Dietary Patterns, Physical Conditioning
License & Citation: Palechor & de la Hoz Manotas (Data in Brief, 2019)
