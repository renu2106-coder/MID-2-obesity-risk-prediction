Executive Summary & System Overview
Healthcare Machine Learning Practical Demonstration

1. Problem Context: Obesity is a complex multifactorial condition associated with cardiometabolic risk. This project evaluates the capacity of Machine Learning models to classify an individual's obesity tier using behavioral lifestyle indicators, demographic attributes, and anthropometric measures.

2. Methodological Rigor: Data leakage was strictly prevented by structuring all transformations inside Scikit-learn Pipelines fitted solely on training folds within stratified 5-fold cross-validation.

3. Target Leakage Insights: By designing two controlled experiments (Experiment A excluding anthropometrics vs. Experiment B including height and weight), the system demonstrates that while Random Forest achieves 83.97% accuracy purely on diet, activity, and family history, the rise to 97.85% in Experiment B is primarily a mechanistic consequence of the mathematical definition of WHO BMI thresholds.

4. Educational Governance: Predictions are transparently accompanied by all seven class probabilities, local feature importance drivers, and unequivocal statements clarifying that statistical model outputs are educational estimates and do not constitute clinical diagnoses.
