Methodological Exploration: Target Leakage (Experiment A vs. Experiment B)
Evaluating model performance when anthropometric proxies are excluded vs. included
Academic Insight: The target classes in this dataset were derived from WHO BMI thresholds (which depend on Height and Weight). When Height, Weight, and BMI are included (Experiment B), the model achieves near-deterministic classification (~98%) by learning the BMI formula. When restricted solely to lifestyle and demographic behaviors (Experiment A), the model achieves ~84% accuracy. High accuracy in Experiment B does not prove lifestyle causality.
